# RULES.md outranks the brief, ChatGPT, and anything said in a session

Decided 2026-08-28, at repo creation.

Three documents describe this project and they do not fully agree. Precedence, highest first:

1. **`RULES.md`** — transcribed from the organisers' PDF. Compliance and rubric truth.
2. **`REQUIREMENTS.md`** — what we decided to build, and the decisions still open.
3. **`HACKATHON_BRIEF.md`** — the ChatGPT handoff. Context and intent, explicitly *not* a spec.

**Why:** the brief was written before anyone had read the PDF closely, and it already differs on
at least one scored point (case count — see [[constraint-ten-case-target]]). Without a stated
precedence, a plausible-sounding line from the brief quietly overrides an actual competition
requirement, and nobody notices until judging.

**How to apply:**
- A compliance question means reading `RULES.md` in full and quoting it, not recalling it.
- When the brief and the PDF conflict, follow the PDF and note the conflict in
  `docs/LEAD-ENGINEER-REVIEW.md` rather than silently picking one.
- If the organisers clarify something in writing, update `RULES.md` and date the change; do not
  keep the correction only in a session.
